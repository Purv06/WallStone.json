WallStone

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

1. Introduction

WallStone is developed and published by Raj Arya Designs.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how WallStone handles information when you use the application.

WallStone is designed to work without requiring an account and does not directly collect personally identifiable information from you.

2. Information We Collect

WallStone does not directly collect personal information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Account or login credentials
  • Contacts
  • Location information
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Advertising ID
  • Android device identifiers such as Android ID or IMEI
  • Payment information

WallStone does not provide account creation, login, or social sign-in functionality.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

WallStone stores certain information locally on your device to provide its features.

  • Wallpaper favorites
  • Wallpaper remix settings and parameters
  • Selected app theme
  • Auto Wallpaper settings
  • Wallpaper rotation interval
  • Auto Wallpaper target
  • Locally cached wallpaper images

This information is stored locally within the app and is not uploaded by WallStone to our own servers.

4. Wallpaper Downloads and GitHub

WallStone retrieves its wallpaper catalog and wallpaper images from publicly accessible GitHub-hosted resources.

The app may make network requests to retrieve wallpaper catalog information, wallpaper names, authors, collections, and image files.

WallStone does not intentionally send personal information to GitHub.

However, when your device connects to GitHub or other internet resources, standard network information such as your IP address may be visible to the relevant hosting provider as part of normal internet communication.

WallStone itself does not read, store, or intentionally transmit your IP address.

5. How We Use Information

Locally stored information is used only to provide WallStone's features, including:

  • Saving favorite wallpapers
  • Remembering appearance preferences
  • Remembering Auto Wallpaper settings
  • Creating and storing wallpaper remixes locally
  • Caching wallpapers for faster loading and offline reuse
  • Applying wallpapers to your device

6. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of WallStone does not contain advertising or analytics services.

  • AdMob / Google Mobile Ads
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Google Analytics
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Crash-reporting services
  • Advertising ID collection
  • Push-notification services

7. Firebase and Cloud Services

WallStone does not currently use Firebase services.

There is no Firebase Authentication, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config, or Firebase database used by the current version of the app.

8. Email and External Links

If you choose to use the Feedback option, WallStone opens your device's default email application with an email addressed to our support contact.

WallStone itself does not transmit the email.

When you open social media links, Play Store links, donation links, or other external services from WallStone, those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Saving Wallpapers to Your Gallery

When you choose to save a wallpaper to your device gallery, WallStone uses Android's standard storage APIs to save the image.

On supported Android versions, wallpapers are saved to the Pictures/WallStone location.

Files saved to your public device gallery may remain on your device even after WallStone is uninstalled. You can remove those files manually through your device's gallery or file manager.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

WallStone does not maintain a personal user account or a server-side user profile.

Information stored locally by WallStone remains on your device until it is removed by the app, cleared through Android's app storage controls, or removed when the app is uninstalled.

Uninstalling WallStone removes its private app data, including locally stored preferences, database information, cached files, and locally stored remix files.

Wallpapers that you have explicitly saved to your public device gallery are not automatically deleted when WallStone is uninstalled.

11. Data Security

WallStone is designed to minimize data collection.

The app does not maintain a server-side database containing personal user information.

Network requests used to retrieve wallpaper content are made over HTTPS where supported by the configured remote resources.

Local app data is stored using Android's private application storage mechanisms.

12. Children's Privacy

WallStone does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

The app does not require an account, name, email address, location, or other personal information to use its core features.

13. Third-Party Services

WallStone may connect to third-party services such as GitHub when retrieving wallpaper catalog data and images.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies. WallStone does not control the privacy practices of external services.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when WallStone's features, services, or data practices change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published at this Privacy Policy URL.

The Effective Date at the top of this policy indicates when the current version became effective.
